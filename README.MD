Bank Loan Analysis & Risk Assessment Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor portfolio performance, assess credit risk, and evaluate loan collection trends across regional branches.

Executive Overview
* Key Metrics: Tracks total applications , total loan amount , disbursed amount , overall approval rate , and default rate.
* Disbursement Trends: Analyzes monthly loan disbursement fluctuations from 2022 to 2025.
* Product Performance: Highlights top-performing loan categories, including Personal, Home, and Auto loans.
* Geographic Distribution: Evaluates regional and branch-level performance across key territories.

Credit Risk & Default Analysis
* Risk Breakdown: Monitors non-performing asset (NPA) rates, average Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio , average credit score , and high-risk customer concentration.
* Product Risk: Visualizes default rates across specific credit offerings like Credit Lines, Home Loans, and Personal Loans.
* Segmentation: Features risk classification (High, Medium, Low, Very High) mapped geographically by state.

Repayment & Customer Insights
* Collection Metrics: Tracks collection rate , total EMI due , total amount paid , missed payments, and late payment rates.
* Customer Segmentation: Analyzes applicant distribution across income tiers (Middle, Upper Middle, Low, High Income).
* Purpose & Status: Breaks down loan purposes (e.g., Home Purchase, Business, Medical) and monitors repayment statuses (Paid, Paid Late, Missed, Partially Paid).
