HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze workforce demographics, track employee attrition, and identify key drivers influencing employee turnover across departments[cite: 5].

Key Highlights
* Core Workforce Metrics: Tracks total employees , active headcount , overall attrition count , average employee age , and national attrition rate .
* Department & Role Breakdown: Identifies attrition distribution across key departments (R&D, Sales, HR) and measures job role satisfaction scores.
* Demographic Insights: Analyzes employee age bands, gender splits, and education levels (Bachelor's, Master's, Associate's, High School, Doctoral).
* Attrition Analysis: Measures turnover rates cross-referenced by age groups and gender to uncover targeted retention insights.
